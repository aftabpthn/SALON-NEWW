# Code of Conduct

## Our Standards

We build in a professional, respectful environment.
All contributors should:
- Communicate respectfully and avoid personal attacks.
- Keep discussions focused on technical and business outcomes.
- Share concerns early, with clear reproduction steps and expected behavior.
- Respect user data privacy, tenant boundaries, and security practices.
- Escalate critical production issues immediately in the team channel.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, abuse, threats, or humiliating comments.
- Discrimination or exclusion based on identity or background.
- Publishing or sharing confidential credentials, secrets, customer data, or production secrets.
- Willful data corruption, destructive commands, or bypassing approval norms.
- Repeatedly ignoring security rules in `AGENTS.md`, especially protected file constraints.

## Reporting

If you see a policy violation, report to the project lead and include:
- Date/time
- What happened
- Impact
- Recommended immediate fix

We review and act based on severity and potential customer impact.
